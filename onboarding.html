Onboarding — Retail Banking

Open a new Bank Islam retail account online. Follow four guided steps — from web form to your signed offer letter — with document review and approval handled digitally.

Fully digital, no branch visit Shariah-compliant Docusign-powered review & signature
The Journey

Four steps from application to account

Each step below is tracked automatically — you'll be notified as your application moves from submission to approval.

1
Step 1

Webform 작성
Complete the web form

Applicant fills in personal details, contact information, employment details and the retail product they'd like to open — all through a guided online web form.

  • Personal & contact details
  • Employment & income information
  • Product selection
2
Step 2

Data Processing & Submission
Verify, sign & submit documents

Submitted information is processed and shown back to the applicant for confirmation. The applicant signs and uploads the required supporting documents.

  • Review & confirm submitted data
  • e-Signature on consent forms
  • Upload required documents (ID, proof of address, income)
3
Step 3

Bank Review
Internal review & approval

Bank Islam's back-office team reviews the application and documents internally, running compliance and eligibility checks before granting approval.

  • Document & data verification
  • Compliance & eligibility checks
  • Internal approval decision
4
Step 4

Offer Signature
Sign your Retail Banking offer

Once approved, the applicant receives the Retail Banking offer letter and signs electronically to complete account opening.

  • Digital offer letter issued
  • Final e-signature
  • Account activated
Before You Start

What you'll need

Documents

  • NRIC / Passport Required
  • Proof of address Required
  • Latest payslip / income proof Required
  • Employment letter If self-employed

Good to know

  • Estimated time to complete ~15 minutes
  • Bank review turnaround 1–2 business days
  • Signature method Docusign e-signature
  • Applicant age 18 years & above
